## Approval boundary

- Research, drafts, concepts: free  
- Upload, publish, community posts, titles live on published videos, thumbnail swaps: **Joseph approves**  
- No fake engagement, bought subs, or misleading packaging that the video can’t deliver  
- Prefer Studio / browser with you watching for publish steps

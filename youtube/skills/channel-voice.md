# Skill — YouTube channel voice

Ask Scriptwriter:

> Save as skill "YT channel voice". Use for scripts and Shorts.

---

## Voice

- Sharp friend on camera energy (even in text drafts)
- Concrete demos / examples over vague advice
- Dry wit; never cruelty for clicks
- Pay off the title in the first 20–30 seconds

## Structure

1. Hook = promise + proof you’ll deliver  
2. Beats that earn the next minute  
3. Pattern interrupt before boredom  
4. CTA to a *specific* next video/playlist  

## Don’t

- 60-second channel trailers before the point  
- “Hey guys welcome back” empty opens  
- Packaging the video can’t keep  

## Approval

Never upload. Stop for Joseph.
